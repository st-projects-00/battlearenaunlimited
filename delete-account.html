Delete your Battle Arena account

For the Battle Arena Android game by Battle Arena Unlimited (package com.battlearenaunlimited.game)

You can ask us to delete your Battle Arena account and everything associated with it at any time. You do not need to keep the app installed to make the request.

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email to battlearenaunlimited@gmail.com with the subject line Delete my Battle Arena account.
  2. Send it from the email address registered to the account. This is how we verify the request is yours. If you no longer have access to that address, say so in the email and include your display name; we will ask a couple of questions to confirm it is your account.
  3. We reply to confirm and delete the account within 30 days of verifying the request. You will receive a final email once it is done.
Deletion is permanent. Progress, unlocked characters and in-game currency cannot be restored afterwards.

What is deleted

DataWhat happens
Account: email address, display name, passwordDeleted
Game progress: level, experience, credits, gems, unlocked characters and itemsDeleted
Social data: friends, friend requests, messagesDeleted
Match history and results linked to the accountDeleted
Sign-in sessions and tokensDeleted, signing you out on every device

What is kept, and for how long

Nothing is kept about you once deletion completes. Our database removes every record linked to the account together, so there is no partial state.

Encrypted database backups may contain a copy of the deleted data for up to 30 days after deletion, after which they expire automatically. Backups are never used to restore an individual account and are not accessible from the game.

Data held by Epic Online Services for multiplayer sessions is tied to a device identifier rather than to your Battle Arena account, and is handled under the Epic Games Privacy Policy.

Deleting only some data

If you would rather keep the account and remove specific data — for example your friends list or message history — email the same address and say what you want removed. We will do that within the same 30 days.
