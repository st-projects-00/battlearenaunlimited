Battle Arena Privacy Policy

Effective 10 September 2026

Battle Arena is a multiplayer mobile game. This policy explains what information the game collects, why, and what happens to it. It applies to the Android app published under the package name com.battlearenaunlimited.game.

Information we collect

You create an account to play. When you do, and as you play, the game stores the following on our servers:

InformationWhy we collect it
Email addressTo identify your account, let you sign in, and send a password reset if you ask for one.
PasswordTo secure your account. It is stored only as a salted hash (argon2id); we cannot read it.
Display nameShown to other players in matches.
Game progressLevel, experience, in-game currency, unlocked characters and items, and match results, so your progress persists between sessions.
Social dataFriend requests, friendships, and messages between friends, where those features are used.
Session tokensTo keep you signed in. Stored hashed and expired automatically.

The game does not collect your location, contacts, photos, microphone audio, or the list of apps on your device, and it does not use an advertising identifier. There are no advertisements and no analytics or tracking libraries in the app.

Information shared with third parties

Epic Online Services (Epic Games, Inc.) provides the multiplayer session and matchmaking service. To connect players into a match, the app sends a device identifier and session data to Epic. Epic's handling of that data is described in the Epic Games Privacy Policy.

Google Cloud hosts our servers and database. Google processes the data on our behalf and does not use it for its own purposes.

We do not sell your information, and we do not share it with anyone else.

In-game currency

Credits and gems are earned by playing and spent within the game. They are not purchased with real money, have no cash value, and cannot be exchanged or refunded.

How your information is protected

All communication between the app and our servers is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Passwords are never stored in readable form. Session tokens are single-use and rotate on each refresh, so a stolen token can be used at most once.

How long we keep it

We keep your account data for as long as your account exists. Match results are kept so that progress and rewards can be verified.

Deleting your account and data

You can request deletion of your account and all associated data by emailing battlearenaunlimited@gmail.com from the address registered to the account. We will delete the account and everything linked to it — progress, social data and match history — and confirm by reply. The full steps, and exactly what is removed, are on the account deletion page. An in-app deletion option is planned.

Children

Battle Arena is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us at the address above and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this address with a new effective date.
